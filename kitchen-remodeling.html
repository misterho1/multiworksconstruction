Kitchen Remodeling in Utah.

Custom kitchens engineered around how you actually cook, host and live — not a stock layout pulled from a showroom.

What this is

A better way to do kitchen remodeling.

A kitchen remodel is a high-stakes investment in your daily life and your home's resale value. We approach kitchens as a craft: layout first, then cabinetry, then appliances and finishes. Whether you want a transitional white-and-brass classic, a moody English cottage kitchen or a fully modern chef's space, our team plans, builds and installs every component.

Scope of work

What's included in our kitchen remodeling service.

Every project is custom, but here's what most kitchen remodeling engagements with Multiworks include from kickoff to final walk-through:

  • Layout redesign, island reconfiguration, wall removal
  • Fully custom cabinetry — paint-grade, stain-grade or quarter-sawn hardwood
  • Stone, quartz and porcelain countertops, full-height backsplashes
  • Built-in appliance packages (Wolf, Sub-Zero, Miele, Thermador)
  • Plumbing, electrical, gas and HVAC reworks to support new layouts
  • Hood vents, pantry build-outs, butler's pantries, beverage stations
  • Lighting design — recessed, under-cabinet, decorative pendants

Common questions

Kitchen Remodeling — frequently asked.

Most full kitchen remodels take 10–16 weeks from demo to final punch. Cabinetry lead times drive the schedule — we order early and stage delivery to minimize the time you're without a kitchen.
