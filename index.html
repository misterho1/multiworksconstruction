How we work

The Multiworks process.

01
Consult
We meet at your property or our office, walk the project, listen carefully, and put a realistic budget and timeline on the table, usually inside a week.
02
Design
Plans, elevations, 3D renderings and a written specification. You see exactly what you're getting before a single dollar of construction is spent.
03
Build
One project manager, one schedule, dedicated trades. Bi-weekly updates with photos, milestones and any change orders signed before work proceeds.
04
Hand-off
Final walk-through, punch list completion, warranty package, and a complete project handbook covering every system, finish, and serial number installed.
